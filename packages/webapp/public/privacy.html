BlitzOS privacy notice

BlitzOS uses information needed to create your account, provide a cloud workspace, and keep the service working. Google supplies sign-in identity data. Workspace content is processed to provide the workspace you request. This notice describes those basic uses.

What account information is used?

When you sign in with Google, BlitzOS receives your Google account identifier, email address, name, and avatar for the sign-in flow. BlitzOS uses it to create and secure your account and connect you to your workspace.

What workspace information is processed?

BlitzOS processes the files, terminal input, agent sessions, and preview traffic needed to provide the workspace features you use. Do not place information in a workspace unless you are allowed to process it there.

Why is information used?

Information is used to provide the service, authenticate access, maintain reliability, prevent abuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Who else processes information?

Google supports sign-in. BlitzOS may use other providers needed to run the service or disclose information when required by law.

What choices do users have?

You can stop using the service. You may ask BlitzOS about access, correction, or deletion. Rights and available responses depend on applicable law and on information BlitzOS must retain.

How can this notice change?

BlitzOS may update this notice as the service changes. The updated date on this page shows the current version.
